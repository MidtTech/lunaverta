Remove the location from photos before you share them

Most phone photos quietly record where they were taken. A photo of the sofa you are selling can tell a stranger where you live. Here is how to remove that before you share.

Step by step

  1. Open Remove photo location.
  2. Choose your photos, or drop them on the page.
  3. The location, camera details, dates and other hidden data are removed. The picture itself is not touched, so there is no loss of quality.
  4. Save the cleaned photos and share those instead of the originals.

Doesn't the website remove it anyway?

Some big sites remove the location when you upload, but you cannot always tell, and photos sent by e-mail, as files in a chat or on a USB stick usually keep it. Cleaning them first means you do not have to rely on anyone else.

Turn it off for future photos

  • iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Camera › Never.
  • Android: open the Camera app, go to its settings and switch off location tags (the name varies by phone).

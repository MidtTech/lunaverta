Make a PDF small enough to e-mail

Your PDF is too big to attach. Most mail services stop at 20 to 25 MB, and some companies set an even lower limit. Here is how to make it small enough.

Step by step

  1. Open Compress PDF and choose the PDF.
  2. Every page is redrawn more compactly. The text stays searchable and can still be copied.
  3. You see how much smaller it became. Click Save.
  4. If it could not get smaller, you get your original back unchanged.

What makes PDFs big

Almost always pictures: scans, photos and phone snaps of paper. A 30-page scan can easily be 40 MB; after compressing it is often a few MB. PDFs that are mostly typed text are usually small already, and compressing them does little.

Still too big?

  • Split it: Split PDF makes one file per page, and Extract pages lets you send only the pages that matter.
  • Send it in parts, one attachment per e-mail.

When not to compress

Compressing turns the pages into pictures with the text kept on top, so links and fillable form fields are not kept. If the receiver has to fill in or sign the form, send the original.

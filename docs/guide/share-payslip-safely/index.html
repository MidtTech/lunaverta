Share a payslip safely

Landlords, banks and new employers often ask to see a payslip. They need your income, not your CPR number or bank account. Here is how to send only what they need.

Step by step

  1. Open Anonymise PDF and choose the payslip.
  2. Your CPR number, account numbers, e-mail and phone number are found and marked in yellow.
  3. Keep what the receiver needs to see. If the pay amounts are marked, switch the Prices category off so they stay.
  4. Click Remove … and save new version, and send the saved copy.

What to remove, and what to keep

  • Remove: CPR number, bank account and registration number, tax card details you were not asked for, your home address if the receiver already has it.
  • Keep: your name, the employer, the period and the pay amounts. That is usually what the receiver needs to check.

Several months?

If you are asked for three months, anonymise each payslip, then put them together with Merge PDF so you only send one file.

Turn iPhone photos (HEIC) into JPG

iPhones save photos as HEIC to save space. That is fine on the phone, but many Windows PCs, websites and forms will not take them. Here is how to turn them into ordinary JPG photos.

Step by step

  1. Open HEIC to JPG.
  2. Choose your HEIC photos, or drop them on the page. You can take many at once.
  3. They are turned into JPG straight away, on your device.
  4. Click Save on each one, or save them all at once as a .zip.

Is the quality the same?

The photo looks the same. JPG files are a bit bigger than HEIC, which is the price of being readable everywhere. If you need them smaller, for example for a web form with a size limit, use Compress image afterwards.

Location and other hidden data

Photos from a phone usually carry the place they were taken. If you are sharing them with strangers, run them through Remove photo location as well.

Stop the iPhone making HEIC

On the iPhone, go to Settings › Camera › Formats and choose Most Compatible. New photos are then saved as JPG. Photos you have already taken stay HEIC.

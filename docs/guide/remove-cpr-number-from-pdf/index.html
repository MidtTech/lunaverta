Remove a CPR number from a PDF

A CPR number on a document you share can be misused. Here is how to remove it properly, so it is really gone from the file and not just hidden.

Step by step

  1. Open Anonymise PDF and choose your PDF.
  2. LunaVerta looks through every page and marks CPR numbers in yellow, along with other things people usually remove, such as account numbers, e-mails and phone numbers.
  3. If you only want to remove the CPR number, switch the other categories off. Tap a marking to keep that piece of text.
  4. Click Remove … and save new version. The marked text is deleted from the file, and a copy is saved on your device. Your original is not changed.

Why a black box in Word or a PDF reader is not enough

Drawing a black rectangle over a number usually only covers it. The text is still underneath, and anyone can copy it out or find it with a search. LunaVerta deletes the letters themselves, so there is nothing left to copy.

Check the result

Open the saved PDF, press Ctrl+F (or Cmd+F on a Mac) and search for the first digits of the number. Nothing should be found.

Scanned documents

If the PDF is a scan (a picture of paper), there is no text to search. Use Black out in the same window and drag a box over the number yourself; it is burned into the picture when you save.

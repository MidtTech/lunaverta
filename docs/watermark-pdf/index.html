Questions about Watermark PDF

Can the watermark be removed again?

It is drawn onto every page of the new PDF, so it can't simply be clicked away. Keep your original if you need a clean copy.

Can I add a stamp instead?

Yes. The same window has stamps such as Approved or Paid that you place on a single page.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. LunaVerta has no server that receives files. Every conversion runs in your browser, on your device, and the result is saved straight to your downloads.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the app is stored on your device. Helpers for PDF, Word, Excel, MP3 and HEIC are saved the first time you use them, then work offline too.

What does it cost?

Nothing. There are no accounts, ads, cookies or trackers.

Why is it free?

Because everything runs on your own device, LunaVerta has almost no running costs: there are no servers that receive, store or process files. That is why it can be free without ads, without accounts and without selling data. LunaVerta is made by Midt-Tech in Aarhus as a useful tool and a showcase of what we build. If it saves you time, you are welcome to (MobilePay, Denmark only for now).

Will LunaVerta stay free?

Yes, that is the plan. LunaVerta costs very little to run, so there is no reason to charge for it today. If you find it useful and would like to support the work, you are welcome to . As the platform grows, some features may later be offered as part of an optional subscription, but there are no such plans at the moment.

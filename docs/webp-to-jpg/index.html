Questions about WebP to JPG

Can I convert to PNG instead?

Yes. Pick PNG in the ring or under Convert all to.

Is it free?

Yes, with no limits, no account and no watermark.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. LunaVerta has no server that receives files. Every conversion runs in your browser, on your device, and the result is saved straight to your downloads.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the app is stored on your device. Helpers for PDF, Word, Excel, MP3 and HEIC are saved the first time you use them, then work offline too.

What does it cost?

Nothing. There are no accounts, ads, cookies or trackers.

About LunaVerta

LunaVerta is a free file converter and PDF editor that runs entirely in the web browser. Files are processed on the user's own device and are never uploaded to a server. It works offline after the first visit, needs no account, sets no cookies and shows no ads. LunaVerta is made in Denmark by Midt-Tech, and its code is open source under the MIT License.

How to Scan to PDF

  1. Press Choose files and take a photo, or choose photos you already have.
  2. Check the four corners, pick colour, grey or black and white, and add more pages.
  3. Press Make PDF. The PDF is saved on your device.

Questions about Scan to PDF

Does it work on a computer too?

Yes. Choose photos or scans from your computer, and they are straightened and turned into a PDF the same way.

The corners are wrong. What do I do?

Drag the four round handles onto the corners of the paper. It works best with the paper on a darker surface.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Everything, including the text recognition, happens on your device.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. LunaVerta has no server that receives files. Every conversion runs in your browser, on your device, and the result is saved straight to your device.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the app is stored on your device. Helpers for PDF, Word, Excel, MP3 and HEIC are saved the first time you use them, then work offline too.

What does it cost?

Nothing. There are no accounts, ads, cookies or trackers.

Why is it free?

Because everything runs on your own device, LunaVerta has almost no running costs: there are no servers that receive, store or process files. That is why it can be free without ads, without accounts and without selling data. LunaVerta is made by Midt-Tech in Aarhus as a useful tool and a showcase of what we build. If it saves you time, you are welcome to (MobilePay, Denmark only for now).

Will LunaVerta stay free?

Yes, that is the plan. LunaVerta costs very little to run, so there is no reason to charge for it today. If you find it useful and would like to support the work, you are welcome to . As the platform grows, some features may later be offered as part of an optional subscription, but there are no such plans at the moment.

About LunaVerta

LunaVerta is a free file converter and PDF editor that runs entirely in the web browser. Files are processed on the user's own device and are never uploaded to a server. It works offline after the first visit, needs no account, sets no cookies and shows no ads. LunaVerta is made in Denmark by Midt-Tech.

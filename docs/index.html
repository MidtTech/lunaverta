Questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. LunaVerta has no server that receives files. Every conversion runs in your browser, on your device, and the result is saved straight to your downloads.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the app is stored on your device. Helpers for PDF, Word, Excel, MP3 and HEIC are saved the first time you use them, then work offline too.

Can I install it?

In Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar. Once installed, you can open files with LunaVerta from your file manager. On iPhone and iPad, use Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Is there a size limit?

Only your device's memory. Large videos and long audio take longer and use more memory, so convert them a few at a time.

What does it cost?

Nothing. There are no accounts, ads, cookies or trackers.

Can it remove the location from my photos?

Yes. Choose Clean for a photo, and LunaVerta removes the GPS location, camera details and other hidden data without re-saving the image, so the quality stays the same.

Why can't it convert video to MP4 or open DOC files?

Those need large engines like FFmpeg and LibreOffice that are heavy to run in a browser. LunaVerta sticks to conversions that run quickly and reliably on any device.

Can I edit a PDF?

Yes. Choose Edit for a PDF to reorder, rotate or delete pages, add pages from other PDFs, correct existing text, write new text on a page, and fill in the PDF's form fields. The result is saved as a new PDF.
